# The Architecture of Boredom · 《无聊的建筑》

A bilingual cinematic essay on boredom as a biological signal — dopamine prediction systems, the industrialization of attention, hyperreality, post-modern meaning collapse, and the AI-era future of human cognition.

> "Boredom is not empty. It is a signal that your mind can no longer find reality."
> 「无聊不是空虚。它是一个信号 —— 你的大脑已经找不到现实。」

## Structure (7 chapters + opening + coda)

1. **Biology** — boredom as a low-fuel light on a 200,000-year-old dashboard; dopamine prediction-error cycle, novelty bias, habituation, motivational collapse.
2. **Attention** — the most efficient extraction rig ever pointed at a primate; infinite scroll, variable reward, surveillance economics.
3. **Reality** — five Reality-vs-Feed comparators (meal, travel, friendship, thinking, silence). The simulation only has to be more comfortable than the original.
4. **History** — 50,000 BCE → 2030+. Information density rose six orders of magnitude; the nervous system did not change.
5. **Philosophy** — Schopenhauer, Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Heidegger, Camus, Baudrillard, McLuhan, Byung-Chul Han.
6. **AI Era** — seven questions the AI era is asking; what becomes meaningful when models can generate everything.
7. **Escape** — twelve acts the extraction industry cannot price + a civilizational checklist.
